## 7. School fees collected through the Service — read this

**EduGear never holds the School's money.**

Where the School enables online payments, fees paid by parents settle **directly into the School's
own bank account**, through a dedicated Paystack subaccount in the School's name. Money does not
pass through any account of ours at any point.

Consequently:

- **We take no percentage of tuition.** Our revenue is the subscription fee in clause 6 and
  nothing else.
- **The School must configure its own settlement account** before it can accept card or transfer
  payments. There is no fallback: a school without a configured subaccount cannot take online
  payments through the Service, because the only possible fallback would be settling into our
  account, which we will not do.
- **Paystack's own processing charges are borne by the School**, and are deducted by Paystack from
  each transaction. This keeps the amount a parent pays equal to the amount invoiced.
- **We do not store the School's bank account number.** Paystack holds it. We keep the bank name,
  the account name the bank returned, and the last four digits, so a bursar can recognise the
  account.
- The School's relationship with Paystack is governed by Paystack's own terms, which the School
  accepts separately.

### Refunds

Any refund of school fees is **a matter between the School and the parent**, settled off EduGear,
because the money is already in the School's bank account and never was in ours. The Service
records that a refund happened so that the parent's balance and statement are correct. We are not a
party to it and have no ability to reverse a settled payment.
